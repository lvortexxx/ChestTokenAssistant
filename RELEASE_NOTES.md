**English** · Run **`ChestTokenAssistant.exe`** and answer the 4 quick questions. Nothing to install. Coming from Chest Counter? Your history is taken over automatically.

> **Turn on replays in Fortnite first**: *Settings → Game → Replays → Record Replays: On*. The tool only reads these replay files: no game modification, no access to the game's memory, and it never goes online.

**Chest Token Assistant 0.1**
- **Dashboard**: everything in one window with 8 tabs (Home, Token, Items, Squad, Files, Matches, Settings, Help), each part with a one-line explanation; the console stays as a backup
- **Items tab**: every reward of week 1, week 2 and the whole season with its picture, odds and your chance; tick what you got (saved), ticked by itself when your replays show it, add later weeks yourself
- **New name and look**: Chest Token Assistant by LVortexxx, with the "Vortex" theme and a new icon
- **Pirate Shop token odds**: your chance of the token and the weekly treasures, targets, pace and countdown (Token tab)
- **Teammates' chests** counted apart from your own total ("seen" in your replay, a minimum)
- **Squad files**: export yours, import your friends' (double-click or drop it on the dashboard), exact counts for the matches you played together
- **Files tab**: every friend's file you saved and what it changes (chests your replay missed, token chance with and without it)
- **Treasure Hours** (boosted odds) added in the settings, with a notification 10 minutes before; optional **Token** line on the overlay
- **Stats image for Discord** (copy and paste, or save it) and a **daily chest goal** (progress on Home and the overlay)
- **Wide overlay**: total, current match, today's goal bar, token and what today added, next milestone, and new records as they happen
- **New high-resolution icon** and a livelier dashboard (smooth tabs, numbers that count up, shine on new items)
- **4 languages**: French, English, Moroccan Darija and Spanish
- **Old versions**: older copies of this assistant still running are closed at each start; *Settings → Stop old versions* also closes ChestCounter.exe / ChestTracker.exe and stops them from opening with Fortnite
- **Never goes online**: the update check is gone, and the dashboard is shown to your own PC only

Windows may show *"Windows protected your PC"* (unsigned exe): *More info → Run anyway*.
